# Mobile Deal Finder Guide

## What this adds

A phone-friendly tool for finding free and underpriced items to resell.

It helps you:

- Open saved Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist searches
- Manually save promising listings
- Score each listing for profit potential
- Draft seller messages
- Track pickup/resale status
- Track estimated profit

## Important compliance rule

This tool does **not** scrape Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist.

It opens searches for you and lets you manually import listings. This avoids prohibited scraping, spam, CAPTCHA bypassing, or automated seller messaging.

## How to use from your phone

1. Start the bot.
2. Open the Streamlit dashboard on your phone.
3. Go to **Mobile Deal Finder**.
4. Tap a saved search.
5. Copy promising listing details back into the app.
6. Let the app score the deal.
7. Use the generated seller message.
8. Update the status as you go.

## Add to your phone home screen

### iPhone Safari

1. Open the dashboard URL.
2. Tap Share.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

### Android Chrome

1. Open the dashboard URL.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**.
