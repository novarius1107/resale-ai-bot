# Safety and Compliance Notes

This project is built for compliant assistance, not deceptive automation.

Do not use it to:
- Spam customers, groups, forums, or marketplaces
- Create fake reviews or fake engagement
- Sell counterfeit, recalled, dangerous, or restricted products
- Bypass platform fees or policies
- Auto-refund, auto-cancel, or auto-close disputes without review
- Scrape websites that prohibit scraping

Use official APIs, permitted exports, affiliate feeds, or other allowed data sources.

Before production use, review:
- Marketplace prohibited/restricted item policies
- Messaging policies
- API terms
- Privacy/data retention rules
- Advertising platform rules
