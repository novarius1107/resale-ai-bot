# Feature Overview

## Trend-to-Listing Engine
- Adds product candidates
- Filters restricted/risky keywords
- Estimates profit after shipping and fees
- Creates draft listings for owner review

## Customer Intake & Priority Engine
- Classifies customer messages
- Assigns priority
- Generates first response
- Creates alerts for high/urgent issues

## Conversation Memory
- Keeps customer threads connected by thread ID
- Stores message summaries, category, priority, and order context

## Auto Follow-Ups
- Schedules follow-ups when customers have not supplied requested details
- Limits follow-ups per thread
- Designed to stop when a real platform integration reports a customer reply

## Message Archive
- Stores all customer and AI messages
- Searchable in dashboard
- Exportable to CSV

## Marketing Intelligence
- Scores promotion opportunities
- Suggests best advertising channels
- Allows only safe owned-channel/SEO automations by default
- Paid ads, outreach, external groups, or community posts require approval
